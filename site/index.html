Check my site

  1. In your WordPress admin, go to Tools → Site Health, then the Info tab.
  2. Click Copy site info to clipboard.
  3. Paste it below and press Check.

What you paste stays in your browser. Only the names and version numbers of WordPress, PHP, your plugins and your themes are sent to two public lookup services (wordpress.org and WPVulnerability). Server paths, user counts and settings are never sent.

About

Made by the webministry of the Barony of Glyn Dwfn so that webministers can check their own sites without needing a security background. This is not an official publication of the Society for Creative Anachronism or the Kingdom of An Tir.

The checker only knows about problems that have been published. A clean result means nothing known applies, not that a site is guaranteed safe. Keep backups, keep things updated, and remove what you don't use.

Vulnerability data from WPVulnerability (public domain, CC0). Plugin, theme and WordPress version information from WordPress.org.
